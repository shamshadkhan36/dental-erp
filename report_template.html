3 Regulatory Compliance & NDC Ethical Advertising (Point 13)
Statutory Compliance Portal & AI Engine Guardrails
Point 13 Implemented
Enforces complete compliance with the National Dental Commission (NDC) and State Dental Council ethical advertising guidelines:
  • Dedicated Terms Portal (app/terms/page.tsx): Full regulatory document with quick navigation in the sidebar and sign-up flows.
  • Interactive Compliance Modal (components/TermsModal.tsx): Accessible directly during sign-up with two distinct tabs:
    • Part A: NDC & State Dental Council Rules: Strict prohibition of superlative claims (e.g., "Best Dentist in Town", "Guaranteed 100% Painless"), mandatory patient informed consent for before/after photos and radiograph scans, transparent credential and fee disclosure rules.
    • Part B: ERP Software Terms: Clear demarcation establishing the registered dental practitioner as the final signing and publishing authority; HIPAA and Google Drive privacy policies.
  • AI Prompt Regulatory Enforcement (lib/ai-prompts.ts): The content generation engine automatically appends the mandatory clinical disclaimer to every generated post:
"*Clinical Disclaimer: Individual treatment outcomes vary. Medical evaluation required. Practice registered under State Dental Council guidelines.*"

9 Database Architecture Recommendation
Component Technology Clinical & Architecture Rationale

Flexible Post Schemas PostgreSQL JSONB Stores multi-platform variations (Instagram captions, Reels scripts, YouTube tags) without schema migrations.
